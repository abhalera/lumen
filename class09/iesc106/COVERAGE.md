### 4. End-of-Chapter Exercises (All 16 Mapped with Full Solutions)
- Q1: Table at constant velocity (Friction = F)
- Q2: Ball on frictionless plane (No force: constant v; Forward force: increases; Opposing: decreases)
- Q3: Block P (4N vs 5N) and Block Q (constant v) $\to$ P experiences net force, Q does not
- Q4: Vallum Kali 100 oarsmen (95 forward, 5 backward) $\to$ 18,000 N
- Q5: Net force accelerates object in direction of force ($a \propto F$)
- Q6: Position-time graphs $\to$ Curved graph A experiences net force
- Q7: Sailor jumping forward $\to$ Boat moves backward (3rd Law)
- Q8: High jump landing mat $\to$ Increases $\Delta t$, decreases impact force
- Q9: Loaded cart vs empty cart collision $\to$ Mutual forces are strictly equal
- Q10: Acceleration-mass graph $\to$ Force-mass graph is horizontal line at $F = 10\text{ N}$
- Q11: Velocity-time graph (10 kg) $\to$ Retarding force of 50 N for first 4s; 0 N for 4–8s
- Q12: 50 g bullet entering wood at 100 m/s stopping in 50 cm $\to$ Retarding force 500 N
- Q13: Football penalty shot (0.4 kg, 108 km/h, 800 N) $\to$ Contact time 15 ms
- Q14: 2 kg object at 10 m/s on rough patch (7 N friction + 3 N force) $\to$ Stopping distance 10 m
- Q15: Tractor pulling harrow and trolley $\to$ Combined acceleration $a = (a_1 a_2)/(a_1 + a_2)$
- Q16: Bar magnet and compass needle $\to$ Equal force, needle moves due to tiny mass/inertia
