## 2. Concept Mapping & NCERT Activity Integration

### Concept 1.1: The Spirit of Exploration — Inquiry, Observation & Scientific Models
- **Textbook Sections:** 1.1 (Pages 1-2)
- **Coverage Details:**
  - Transition from primary curiosity to secondary structured exploration.
  - The Magnifying Glass (rigorous observation and empirical evidence) and the Compass (purposeful direction, model selection, boundaries of validity).
  - Power of scientific models: simplification, deliberate idealization, isolating dominant variables while neglecting minor perturbations.
  - Historical case study: Meghnad Saha's stellar ionization model treating matter as a hot gas governed by temperature and pressure.
  - Projectile modeling of a cricket ball hit for a six (Example 1.1).
- **NCERT Activity:** Activity 1.1 (Modeling bicycle commute travel time from school to home).
- **Interactive Simulation:** `sim-scientific-modeling` (Projectile trajectory comparing ideal vacuum parabola vs quadratic air drag vs Magnus backspin).

### Concept 1.2: The Language of Science — Symbols, SI Units & Mathematical Equations
- **Textbook Sections:** 1.2 (Pages 2-3)
- **Coverage Details:**
  - Precision of scientific vocabulary: distinguishing operational definitions (force, work, reaction, cell) from colloquial usage.
  - The International System of Units (SI metric system) and history of symbols (e.g. $c$ for speed of light from Latin *celeritas*).
  - Engineering case study: The 1983 Gimli Glider incident (Air Canada Flight 143: 22,300 kg vs 22,300 lb causing a 54.6% fuel deficit and mid-flight engine flameout).
  - Mathematics as a thinking language: equations as compact logical statements of relationships between quantities.
- **NCERT Activity:** Ready to Go Beyond Box (Airplane fuel miscalculation analysis).
- **Interactive Simulation:** `sim-units-conversion` (Gimli Glider aircraft fuel tank balancer and metric/imperial flight track simulator).

### Concept 1.3: Laws, Theories & Predictions — Evidence-Based Inquiry vs Misinformation
- **Textbook Sections:** 1.3 (Pages 3-5)
- **Coverage Details:**
  - The epistemological hierarchy: Hypotheses $\to$ Empirical Laws (what happens) $\to$ Explanatory Theories (why it happens).
  - Self-correction in science: theories are never immutable dogmas; evidence supersedes opinion or authority.
  - Predictive power of science: deducing outcomes before experiments can be performed.
  - Formulating measurable, testable scientific questions (Example 1.2: Varsha & Meghna rain prediction using humidity, barometric pressure trends, and wind vectors).
  - Debunking social media pseudoscience: proving that solar eclipses do not cause cooked food toxicity through physical shadow mechanics and biological decay pathways.
- **NCERT Activities:** Example 1.2, Pause & Ponder 1, and Threads of Curiosity (Solar Eclipse Myth).
- **Interactive Simulation:** `sim-weather-prediction` (Multi-variable atmospheric weather observatory and precipitation probability gauge).

### Concept 1.4: Order of Magnitude & Estimation — The Art of Fermi Reasoning
- **Textbook Sections:** 1.4 (Pages 5-6)
- **Coverage Details:**
  - Enrico Fermi's estimation technique: dimensional decomposition and sanity checks.
  - Example 1.3: Calculating human daily respiratory volume at rest (14 breaths/min $\times$ 1,440 min $\approx$ 20,000 breaths; 0.5 L/breath $\approx$ 10,000 L of air/day).
  - Sanity check via party balloon inflation rate (2 L in 20 s $\to$ 8,640 L/day).
  - Household monthly rice consumption for a family of four based on caloric requirements (35-50 kg/month).
  - Pause & Ponder 2: Distinguishing scenarios where rough approximations suffice vs where extreme numerical precision is mandatory.
- **NCERT Activities:** Example 1.3, Ready to Go Beyond (Family rice estimation), and Pause & Ponder 2.
- **Interactive Simulation:** `sim-fermi-estimation` (Interactive respiratory volume explorer and household caloric grain calculator).

### Concept 1.5: Science Without Boundaries — Interdisciplinary Systems & Human Enterprise
- **Textbook Sections:** 1.5 (Pages 6-7)
- **Coverage Details:**
  - The artificial nature of disciplinary borders: nature is an interconnected continuum.
  - Real-world multi-disciplinary challenges: global climate change, sustainable energy, and epidemiology.
  - Pause & Ponder 3: Multi-disciplinary breakdown of everyday technology (pressure cooker: thermodynamics, reaction kinetics, and metallurgy).
  - The N95 respirator mask: Physics (inertial impaction, Brownian diffusion, electrostatic electret capture), Polymer Chemistry (melt-blown polypropylene microfibers), Biology (viral aerosol transmission dynamics), and Mathematics (Navier-Stokes fluid dynamics and filtration efficiency curves).
  - Science as a collaborative, self-correcting human enterprise.
- **NCERT Activities:** Pause & Ponder 3 and Ready to Go Beyond (How does a mask really work?).
- **Interactive Simulation:** `sim-n95-filtration` (Multi-physics aerosol filtration simulator: particle size slider, Brownian diffusion, impaction, and electret charge boost).

## 3. Comprehensive End-of-Chapter Exercises Coverage (1 to 6)

1. **Exercise 1 (Pause & Ponder 1):** Everyday predictions (cricket/weather) vs evidence-based scientific reasoning; confidence intervals and testable physical variables.
2. **Exercise 2 (Activity 1.1 & Example 1.1):** Modeling a bicycle commute to school vs cricket shot trajectory; deliberate idealization and isolating primary physical laws.
3. **Exercise 3 (Gimli Glider Case Study):** Quantitative calculation of fuel deficit (22,300 lb vs kg = 54.6% deficit) and importance of standardized SI units.
4. **Exercise 4 (Example 1.2 & Eclipse Myth):** Formulating four measurable atmospheric variables for rain prediction; debunking the solar eclipse food toxicity superstition.
5. **Exercise 5 (Example 1.3 & Pause & Ponder 2):** Fermi estimation of daily respiratory volume (10,000 L/day); analyzing when approximation suffices vs when exact precision is mandatory.
6. **Exercise 6 (Pause & Ponder 3 & N95 Multi-Physics):** Interdisciplinary breakdown of a pressure cooker; multi-disciplinary science of N95 respirator filtration.
