Shadow 1.6 m · tower 42 m

Medians scale with sides. RHS note p.26.

Triangles (unlike general polygons): one similarity condition implies the other. BPT runs both directions. Lamp-post (4.8+x)/x = 4 gives x = 1.6 m. Pole/tower gives 42 m. Pythagoras proof previewed via altitude; areas of similar triangles not in this rationalised chapter.

  2. Explain why AA suffices (where did the third angle go?).
