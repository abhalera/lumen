SCIENCE / CHEMICAL REACTIONS /
KEEP THE BIG PICTURE

Your chapter, on one page.

Balanced equation

Atoms LHS = atoms RHS

Only coefficients change; never subscripts.
Combination

A + B → AB

Exothermic often: CaO + H2O.
Decomposition

AB → A + B

Needs heat, light or electricity.
Redox

Oxidation +O/−H · Reduction −O/+H

Both happen together, always.

Coefficients count molecules; subscripts define compounds. Rust needs air AND water; decomposition needs energy IN.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Balance Mg + O2 → MgO from a skeletal start and state the rule you obeyed.
  2. Classify Zn + CuSO4 and Pb(NO3)2 + KI with the visible evidence for each.
  3. Explain why chips packets use nitrogen and why iron gates need paint.
Check your explanation

1. 2Mg + O2 → 2MgO; atoms conserved, subscripts untouched. 2. Displacement (blue fades, brown Cu) and double displacement/precipitation (yellow PbI2). 3. N2 blocks fat oxidation (rancidity); paint blocks air + moisture (rust).
