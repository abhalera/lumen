NCERT numbers

Lattu 39.6 · block 163.86 · shed air 827.15

Glass 163.54 · capsule 220 · tent ₹22000.

Surface counts skin with air, so glued faces vanish; volume counts occupied space, so joined bulks add and inserts subtract. Cone height = total − r when a hemisphere caps it; cavities trade a flat disc for a larger curved bowl.
