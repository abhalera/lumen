Secant vs tangent

0, 1 or 2 meetings — never more

Tangent = secant whose chord endpoints coincided.
Theorem 10.1

Radius ⊥ tangent at contact

OQ > OP for every Q ≠ P; OP is shortest.
Theorem 10.2

Two tangents from P are equal

ΔOQP ≅ ΔORP (RHS) → PQ = PR; or Pythagoras.
Measure

PT = √(OP² − r²)

Right angle at contact licenses Pythagoras; ∠PTQ + centre = 180°.

One touch is a tangent, two cuts a secant. The radius meets its tangent at 90°. From outside, exactly two tangents fall — equal in length, with OP bisecting their angle.

  3. ∠POQ = 110°. Find ∠PTQ and name the quadrilateral fact used.
