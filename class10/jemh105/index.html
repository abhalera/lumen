AP test

Gaps all equal d

d can be +, −, or 0.
General form

a, a+d, a+2d, …

a and d fix everything.
nth term

an = a + (n−1)d

n−1 counts jumps. −77, −32.
Sum

Sn = n/2·(2a+(n−1)d)

Pairs each total a+an. 5050, 12600, 4375.

Salary counts by adding; compound savings multiply (not AP). 301 is not a term of 5, 11, … (n = 151/3). Logs: 16 rows, top 5. Trees: 234. Spiral: 143 cm. Penalty 30 days: ₹27750.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Derive Sn by reversal in four lines.
  2. Why do n = 4 and n = 13 both sum 24+21+… to 78?
  3. Why is n = 25 impossible for the 200 logs?
Check your explanation

1. n pairs of a+an, halved. 2. Tail terms 5–13 sum to zero. 3. Rows 21–25 need negative logs.
