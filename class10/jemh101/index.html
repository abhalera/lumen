Decimals

q = 2ⁿ × 5ᵐ ⟺ terminates

Reduce first; leftover rest repeats.

Uniqueness does the work: no hidden primes in 4ⁿ, no stranger in a², no third prime in a terminating denominator. Coprime-first makes contradiction possible; reduce-first makes the decimal rule true.
