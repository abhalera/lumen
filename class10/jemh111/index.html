KEEP THE BIG PICTURE

Your chapter, on one page.

Sector

θ/360 × πr²

Unitary share of the disc.
Arc

θ/360 × 2πr

Same share of circumference.
Segment

Sector − triangle OAB

Minor by default; major = πr² − minor.
Triangle

OM = r·cos(θ/2) · AM = r·sin(θ/2)

Halve 120° → two 60° halves.

Sector needs two radii; segment needs chord + arc. Major angle = 360° − θ. Five minutes = 30°, eight ribs = 45°, corner rope = 90°. Use the π the question states (default 22/7).

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. r = 4, θ = 30°: find the sector and the major sector.
  2. Why is a segment sector − triangle, not a direct θ/360 share?
  3. r = 21, θ = 120°: find OM and AM, then the segment form.
