MATHS / PROBABILITY /
KEEP THE BIG PICTURE

Your chapter, on one page.

Classical

P(E) = favourable / total

Equally likely outcomes assumed (Laplace 1795).
Partitions

Elementary probabilities sum to 1

Heads + tails; Y + R + B; W + B + R.
Complement

P(Ē) = 1 − P(E)

Impossible 0 · sure 1 · 0 ≤ P ≤ 1.
Compounds

Order matters: 4 coin pairs, 36 dice pairs

(1,4) ≠ (4,1) · sum 8: 5/36. Trials → theory; nothing is ever due.

Count the right sample space, partition it honestly, and subtract when the opposite is easier. Theory predicts the long-run share; trials wobble toward it — the next outcome owes you nothing.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Bag: 4 red + 1 blue ball. Find P(red) and name the correct sample space.
  2. Two dice: why is P(sum 8) = 5/36 and not a share of 11 sums?
  3. P(Sangeeta wins) = 0.62. Find P(Reshma wins) and the rule used.
Check your explanation

1. 4/5 — the 5 balls, not the 2 colours. 2. Ordered pairs are equally likely (36); sums are not. 3. 0.38 — complements sum to 1.
