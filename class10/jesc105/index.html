Nutrition

Autotroph: CO2+H2O→food · others: digest in/out

Amoeba vacuole · villi absorb
Energy

Glucose→pyruvate→aerobic ATP (30.5 kJ/mol)

Inhale: ribs up, diaphragm flat
Transport

Double loop · xylem up free, phloem paid both ways

BP 120/80 · arteries→capillaries→veins
Excretion

Filter 180 L → reclaim ~99% → 1–2 L out

Plants: vacuoles, leaf-fall, gums

Food in, energy out, transport between, wastes out: specialised tissues beat diffusion at every step of size.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. A leaf in a KOH bell jar shows no starch: which ingredient went missing and how was the jar sealed?
  2. Trace one blood drop: right atrium to left ventricle naming every stop.
  3. Night transpiration nearly stops: what keeps some xylem flow going, and why can’t it water a tall tree alone?
Check your explanation

1. CO2 absorbed by KOH; vaseline-sealed jar. 2. Right atrium → right ventricle → lungs → left atrium → left ventricle. 3. Root pressure pushes a little; too weak for height — day pull does the lifting.
