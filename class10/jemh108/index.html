Ratios

sin = opp/hyp · cos = adj/hyp · tan = opp/adj

Reciprocals: cosec, sec, cot.
Fixed angle

Same angle → same ratios

AA similarity cancels scale.
Exact values

sin 30° = 1/2 · sin 45° = 1/√2 · sin 60° = √3/2

From half-square, half-equilateral.
Identities

sin²A + cos²A = 1

÷cos² → 1+tan² = sec² · ÷sin² → 1+cot² = cosec²

Ratios belong to angles, not triangles. sin²A = (sin A)². (sin A)⁻¹ = cosec A, but sin⁻¹A is inverse sine (higher classes). Lengths are non-negative: reject negative roots.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. sin A = 1/3. Find cos A and name the theorem used twice.
  2. Explain why tan 90° is undefined, not infinite.
  3. Prove (1+tan²θ)/(1+cot²θ) = tan²θ in two lines.
Check your explanation

1. k-assign + Pythagoras: cos = 2√2/3. 2. tan 90° = 1/0 — division by zero is undefined in this chapter. 3. sec²/cosec² = sin²/cos² = tan²θ.
