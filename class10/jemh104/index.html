Your chapter, on one page.

Standard form

ax² + bx + c = 0, a ≠ 0

Simplify first; degree decides.
Factorise

Split middle (product ac)

Each factor = 0: 2x²−5x+3 → 1, 3/2.
Formula

x = (−b ± √D)/2a

From completing the square; needs D ≥ 0.
Discriminant

D > 0 two · = 0 double · < 0 none

Hall 12 m; pole 5 m; grove pattern.

Roots are zeroes: 2x² − 3x + 1 has 1 and 1/2. ± enters once, at the square-root step. Stories filter roots: −12.5 m, −12 m, −5 cm all die at meaning.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Derive the formula from (x + b/2a)² in three lines.
  2. 2x² − 6x + 3: nature and roots without a calculator.
  3. Why does D = −8 end the discussion for real roots?
Check your explanation

1. Root step gives ±√D/2a. 2. D = 12 > 0: (3 ± √3)/2. 3. No real square is negative.
