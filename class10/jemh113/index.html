Your chapter, on one page.

Mean

Σfixi/Σfi · a + Σfidi/Σfi · a + h·Σfiui/Σfi

Class mark xi; ui = (xi − a)/h.
Mode

l + ((f1−f0)/(2f1−f0−f2)) × h

Inside the modal (max-fi) class.
Median

l + ((n/2 − cf)/f) × h

First cf > n/2 is the median class.
Ogives

Less-than + more-than cross at n/2

Continuous classes; free axis scales.

Grouped mean is approximate (mid-point assumption); assumed/step methods are exact re-writes of direct. Mode leans to the heavier neighbour. Median and ogive share the uniform-spread walk across one class.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Why is grouped mean 62 while ungrouped is 59.3?
  2. Modal class 40–55 (7, neighbours 3, 6): find the mode.
  3. Median class 60–70 (n = 53, cf = 22, f = 7): find the median and say where the ogives cross.
Check your explanation

1. Class marks replace real marks — approximation. 2. 40 + (4/5) × 15 = 52. 3. 60 + (4.5/7) × 10 ≈ 66.4; ogives cross at (66.4, 26.5).
