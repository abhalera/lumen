Your chapter, on one page.

Sight angles

Elevation = above horizontal · Depression = below

Both measured at the observer’s eye.
One triangle

height = baseline × tan(angle)

Add eye height for the total.
Ratio picks

sin → hypotenuse · cot → ground

One unknown per ratio.
Two triangles

One tan equation each, shared side is the hinge

Depression flips to elevation (alternate ∠s).

Angles live at the eye against the horizontal. Triangle gives rise above eye level; add (or subtract) the observer segment. Shared sides carry answers between triangles. Depression at the top = elevation at the bottom.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Baseline 15 m, elevation 60°. Find the height.
  2. Observer 1.5 m, 28.5 m away, elevation 45°. Find the chimney top.
  3. Depression 30° from the top becomes what angle at the bottom, and why?
Check your explanation

1. 15√3 ≈ 25.98 m. 2. Rise 28.5 m + 1.5 m = 30 m. 3. Elevation 30° — transversal cuts parallel horizontals (alternate angles).
