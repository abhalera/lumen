Signalling

Electric inside · chemical between

Decisions

Hormones

Adrenaline: fight/flight · insulin: feedback

Fast signals run on wires, wide signals run on chemistry: cord for reflexes, fore-brain for choices, hormones for the whole body.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Your finger jerks from a thorn before you feel it: name every box of the arc and the brain’s actual role.
  2. A tendril coils while mimosa folds: which grows, which doesn’t, and which hormone steers the coiler?
  3. Sugar high → ? → sugar normal: fill the loop’s two blanks and name the gland.
