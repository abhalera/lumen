Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Name the two structural isomers of butane’s skeleton idea in one line each (straight vs branched).
  2. A stove blackens vessels: name the fault, the missing reactant, and the fix.
  3. Tubewell water gives scum with soap but foam with detergent: explain in two sentences.
Check your explanation

1. n-butane (C–C–C–C) vs iso-butane (branched): same C4H10, different skeleton. 2. Incomplete combustion from choked air; open the air holes for a blue flame. 3. Ca/Mg precipitate soap as scum; detergent salts stay soluble in hard water.
