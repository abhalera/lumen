Light travels left → right in these diagrams. Left distances are negative; right distances positive. Measure from P for a mirror, O for a lens. Above the axis is positive height.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Sketch a concave mirror with an object inside F. Where do the ray extensions meet?
  2. Explain why a ray can leave a glass slab parallel to its original direction, even though it bent twice.
  3. A convex lens has f = +20 cm and u = −30 cm. Find v, m and power.
Check your explanation

1. Behind the mirror: virtual, upright, enlarged. 2. For parallel faces with air on both sides, the two angular changes cancel. At oblique incidence the ray is displaced sideways. 3. 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60; v = +60 cm, m = −2, P = +5 D.
