Indicators

Litmus / methyl orange / phenolphthalein

Colour sorts acid, base, neutral.
Gas tests

Acid+metal → H2 (pop) · carbonate → CO2 (milky)

Confirmatory tests.
pH scale

pH < 7 acid · 7 neutral · > 7 base

Each step = ×10 [H+].
Salts

Strong+strong neutral · stronger parent wins

Brine → NaOH + Cl2 + H2.

Acidity = H3O+ in water; dilution moves pH toward 7 but never across it. Salt pH follows the stronger parent (school model; real salt pH needs measurement).

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Dry HCl does nothing to dry litmus but HCl solution conducts: explain both with ions.
  2. Sort pH 4, 1, 11, 7, 9 into neutral/strong/weak and order by rising [H+].
  3. Trace brine to bleaching powder, baking soda and washing soda with one step each.
Check your explanation

1. No water → no H3O+; dissolved → mobile ions carry current. 2. Neutral 7; strong acid 1, weak acid 4, weak base 9, strong base 11; [H+] rises 11<9<7<4<1. 3. Electrolysis → Cl2+NaOH; Cl2 + dry lime → Ca(ClO)2 (NCERT form; commercial CaOCl2 differs by O count); salt+NH3+CO2 → NaHCO3; heat 2NaHCO3 → Na2CO3 + H2O + CO2 (sodium carbonate), then Na2CO3 + 10H2O → washing soda.
