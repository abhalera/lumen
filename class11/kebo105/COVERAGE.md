## 2. 100% NCERT End-of-Chapter Exercise Solutions

- **Exercise 5.1:** Pinnately vs palmately compound leaf (rachis vs petiole apex attachment, Neem vs Silk cotton).
- **Exercise 5.2:** Phyllotaxy types (alternate in China rose/mustard, opposite in Calotropis/guava, whorled in Alstonia).
- **Exercise 5.3:** Definitions of 7 fundamental botanical terms: aestivation, placentation, actinomorphic, zygomorphic, superior ovary, perigynous flower, epipetalous stamen.
- **Exercise 5.4:** Detailed contrast tables: (a) Racemose vs Cymose inflorescence; (b) Apocarpous vs Syncarpous ovary.
- **Exercise 5.5:** Comprehensive anatomical label description of: (i) Gram seed (dicot non-endospermic); (ii) Vertical section of Maize grain (monocot endospermic, scutellum, coleoptile, coleorhiza).
- **Exercise 5.6:** Complete semi-technical description, floral formula, and floral diagram of Family Solanaceae (*Solanum nigrum* / Brinjal).
- **Exercise 5.7:** Full description and examples of the 5 placentation modes (marginal, axile, parietal with replum, free central, basal).
- **Exercise 5.8:** Morphological definition of flower as modified shoot and parts of the 4 successive floral whorls.
- **Exercise 5.9:** Inflorescence definition and classification basis (indeterminate acropetal racemose vs determinate basipetal cymose).
- **Exercise 5.10:** Arrangement of floral members on thalamus (hypogynous superior ovary, perigynous half-inferior ovary, epigynous inferior ovary).
