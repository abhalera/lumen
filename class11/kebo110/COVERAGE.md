## Chapter Overview
Chapter 10 concludes Unit 3 (Cell Structure and Function) with cell division mechanics:
- 24-hour cell cycle, interphase phases (G1, S, G2), centriole duplication, and G0 quiescent stage.
- Mitosis: Prophase condensation, Metaphase plate alignment, Anaphase centromere splitting, Telophase envelope reassembly.
- Animal cleavage furrow (centripetal) vs plant cell plate (centrifugal middle lamella), syncytium.
- Meiosis I: Leptotene, Zygotene synapsis, Pachytene crossing over via recombinase, Diplotene chiasmata, Diakinesis terminalisation.
- Anaphase I homologous separation without centromere cleavage; Interkinesis without DNA replication.
- Meiosis II equational division, Anaphase II centromere cleavage, tetrad of four genetically unique haploid gametes.
- Genomic accounting: full tracking of chromosome count (N) and DNA mass (C) across every stage.
- Meiotic asymmetry: equal spermatogenesis vs unequal oogenesis.
