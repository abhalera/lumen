## Chapter Overview
Chapter 9 establishes the molecular foundations of living organisms:
- Chemical analysis using trichloroacetic acid: acid-soluble pool (<800 Da) vs acid-insoluble retentate (macromolecules).
- Primary vs secondary metabolites (pigments, alkaloids, terpenoids, toxins, lectins, drugs, polymers).
- Amino acids as substituted methanes and the 4-tier structural hierarchy of proteins (primary, secondary, tertiary 3D fold, quaternary).
- Lipids, fatty acids (palmitic 16C, arachidonic 20C), triglycerides, and membrane phospholipids (lecithin).
- Polysaccharides: linear cellulose, helical starch iodine complex, branched glycogen, chitin NAG.
- Watson-Crick B-DNA double helix geometry, complementary hydrogen bonding, antiparallel strands.
- Enzyme catalysis: active site mechanics, activation energy reduction, Michaelis-Menten kinetics, competitive inhibition (malonate), 6 enzyme classes, and cofactors (prosthetic groups, coenzymes, metal ions).
