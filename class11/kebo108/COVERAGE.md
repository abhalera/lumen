## Chapter Overview
Chapter 8 presents the foundational cytology of living systems:
- History, cell theory, Schleiden & Schwann, Rudolf Virchow (*Omnis cellula-e cellula*).
- Prokaryotic cell architecture (tripartite envelope, mesosomes, plasmids, 70S ribosomes, inclusion bodies).
- Eukaryotic plasma membrane (Fluid Mosaic Model, passive simple diffusion, facilitated carrier transport, active Na+/K+ pumps).
- Endomembrane system coordination (Rough ER, Smooth ER, Golgi cisternae and glycosylation, Lysosomes, Vacuoles and tonoplast).
- Semiautonomous organelles (Mitochondria cristae and matrix, Chloroplasts thylakoids and stroma, circular DNA, 70S ribosomes).
- Cytoskeleton, Cilia/Flagella 9+2 axonemes, Centrosome 9+0 cartwheel centrioles.
- Nucleus (nuclear envelope with pores, nucleolus rRNA synthesis, chromatin packaging) and chromosome morphology (metacentric, submetacentric, acrocentric, telocentric, satellites).
