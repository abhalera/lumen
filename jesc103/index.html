SCIENCE / METALS AND NON-METALS /
KEEP THE BIG PICTURE

Your chapter, on one page.

Properties

Metals bend + conduct; non-metals break

Graphite, Hg, diamond excepted.
Series

K>Na>Ca>Mg>Al>Zn>Fe>Pb>(H)>Cu>Ag>Au

Higher displaces lower.
Ionic bond

Metal loses e-; non-metal gains

High mp; conduct when molten.
Extraction

Heat / carbon / electrolysis by series position

Roast + calcine first.

Reactivity decides burning, water, acids, displacement and extraction route. Oxides: metals basic, non-metals acidic/neutral.

Three-minute exit ticket
  1. Predict: Cu strip in ZnSO4 vs Zn strip in CuSO4, with the rule.
  2. Explain solid vs molten NaCl conduction with ions.
  3. Choose protection for a coastal gate: paint, zinc or stainless — justify.
Check your explanation

1. Cu+ZnSO4: nothing (Cu lower); Zn+CuSO4: ZnSO4+Cu (higher displaces lower). 2. Ions locked in solid, mobile when molten. 3. Galvanised/zinc or stainless for salty air; paint alone fails at scratches.
